# Product Pitch

Most watchlists show you a price. This one tells you whether that price
actually matters. Every tracked stock (US via Finnhub, Indian NSE/BSE via
yfinance) is scored against five weighted signals — price move, volume,
relative-to-market move, breakout, news surge — with a corroboration boost
when several fire together. Below-threshold noise is filtered out; what's
left gets a plain-English explanation (Gemini, guarded against
hallucination with a template fallback) and a live sparkline. Real per-user
accounts mean "what changed since you checked" is a genuine baseline, not a
demo fiction. Dual market-hours polling means Indian symbols aren't starved
outside US hours. Adding a company resolves any real ticker live — not a
hardcoded catalog. The bet: a watchlist's job is triage, not decoration.
